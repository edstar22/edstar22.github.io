    Hi, I'm Edna.

    Communication Studies student at HAN University

    I'm someone whose interests sit at the intersection of nature, technology and culture. I love travelling and meeting new people because of how unique every place and person is. I also love a comfortable environment and learning new things all the time from the comfort of my laptop.

    I live through my senses and appreciate the small things in life: the way a material feels to the touch, the taste of really good food, the way colours influence each other.

    When I work, I strive to design my strategy with all the different ways people could experience it in mind. For me, creating something that can be unique but also appeal to a wide variety of people is one of the greatest puzzles to solve.
